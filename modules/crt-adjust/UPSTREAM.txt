Copied from the Gaiapolis MiSTer port's tree (modules/crt-adjust), which records that commit and carries the one local fix below.

Local change (one line, `hoff_s`): the conditional mixes `$signed(hoffset)` with an unsigned replication, so by the Verilog rules
(and in Verilator) the result is unsigned and a negative H-Position is zero-extended (-1 becomes 511), which moves the picture
window out of the line and blanks the picture. The line now sign-extends `hoffset` explicitly, which is what the module is meant
to do and gives the same result in every tool. Nothing else differs from upstream.
