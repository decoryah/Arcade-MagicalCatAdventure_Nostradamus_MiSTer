Local changes: jt10.v has one more output, adpcmA_l (the ADPCM-A part of the mix, for the diagnostic overlay).

Clocking: the other core runs jt10 at 49 MHz. jt12_div and jt12_rst clock their enables on the falling edge, a half-cycle path
to every flip-flop they enable (10 ns there, 5.2 ns at 96 MHz), so this core runs jt10 on a 48 MHz clock of the same PLL.
